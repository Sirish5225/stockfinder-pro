Guide
About Heatmap
Heatmap is a colour-coded view of index constituents. Green tiles are stocks up; red tiles are stocks down. Stronger colour means a larger move.
How to read it
  • Each tile is one stock in the selected sector or index
  • Green is positive change; red is negative change
  • Click a symbol to open that stock's TradingView chart in a new tab
