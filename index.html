About Sector Heatmap

The Sector Heatmap gives you a quick visual overview of market strength and weakness across stock-market sectors. Each tile shows the sector's average percentage price change for the stocks currently included in your selected market universe.

● Green — Positive Momentum

Sectors with a positive average price change. A stronger reading can help you identify areas showing relative buying interest.

● Red — Negative Momentum

Sectors with a negative average price change. Use these readings to spot weaker areas of the market.

↗ Explore Individual Stocks

Click any sector tile to see its constituent stocks, then use the performance rankings to compare sectors.

Trading tip: Use sector strength as context alongside price action, volume, and demand/supply zones—not as a standalone buy or sell signal. Sector readings reflect the stocks available in the current data set.
