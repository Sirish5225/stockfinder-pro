GUIDE

About Heatmap

Heatmap is a colour-coded view of NSE/BSE sector constituents and market breadth. Green tiles represent stocks that are up; red tiles represent stocks that are down. Stronger colour intensity indicates a larger price move, allowing you to scan market momentum at a glance.

How to read it

  • Each tile represents an individual stock within the selected sector.
  • Green indicates positive price change; Red indicates negative change.
  • Deeper color shades signify stronger momentum and bigger percentage moves.
  • Range filter chips (-3% to +3%) let you hide or isolate specific performance buckets.
  • Clicking any tile or bar filters or opens the respective stock chart for analysis.

How to use it (Intraday Strategy)

  • Scan for color clusters. A block of green points to heavy sector buying; a block of red flags relative weakness.
  • Top-Down Approach: Pick stocks from the strongest sector for Longs, and weakest sector for Shorts.
  • Always look for volume confirmation alongside sector momentum before entering intraday breakout trades.
